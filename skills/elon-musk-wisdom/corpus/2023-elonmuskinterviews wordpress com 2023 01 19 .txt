_Welcome to the second half hour (30:42 – 58:43) of the Ron Baron interview with Elon Musk recorded on November 4, 2022, during the Annual Baron Investment Conference in New York. This part is about the Twitter acquisition, how to logistically get 100 million Teslas a year to customers in the future, why Tesla started with vertical integration in the beginning and how it became a competitive advantage, whether there is a good reason to invest in Tesla, and the long-term goal of Tesla’s superchargers. This transcript is based on the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiMvQSWr-v4) published by [Elon Alerts](https://elon-alerts.com/) on YouTube. A [summary of the Tesla-related content](https://whatsuptesla.com/2022/11/05/tsla/) of this interview has been published by [Gail Alfar](https://twitter.com/GailAlfarATX) on her blog „What’s Up Tesla“. If you’re interested in the transcript of the first half hour of this interview, [here](https://elon-musk-interviews.com/2023/01/13/ron-baron/) you are._
